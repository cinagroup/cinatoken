# C04 text grant lease and acknowledgement audit (v362 design input)

2026-09-25. This is a source audit, not an installed grant or acceptance result. The [v360 complete quote](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/authenticated-complete-text-quote-v360.sql) and [v361 admission](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/complete-text-quote-budget-admission-v361.sql) are review-only. A v361 receipt proves the database reserved the quoted `3 × max` amount at admission time; it does not authorize any physical send.

## Concrete lease trap

The v361 hold expires at the earlier of the quote deadline and 90 seconds after admission (`hold_expires_at`). Its immutable receipt and idempotent branch do not renew a dispatched hold. [`mark_user_budget_dispatched_v350`](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/budget-admission-login-v350.sql) can set an initial dispatched deadline at most 15 minutes after its caller time, but on an already dispatched row it returns `true` without checking that the existing deadline covers the newly requested one. [`mark_guardrail_budgets_dispatched_v351`](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/guardrail-budget-admission-login-v351.sql) rejects a longer replay and does not renew existing dispatched rows. The [v355 extension](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/guardrail-budget-dispatched-extension-login-v355.sql) is specific to a `gateway_key_route` BYOK reservation; v361's Guardrail rows use `charged` basis. Therefore these functions do not provide a general renewal protocol for a text SSE that outlives the initial dispatched lease.

A future grant transaction must inspect every actual ordinary and Guardrail reservation row after any dispatch transition. A returned Boolean alone is insufficient: for example, with no Guardrail source, a second v350 mark can return `true` for a previously dispatched hold that expires sooner than the requested grant lease. The grant's send-start deadline and any recovery deadline must be bounded by the **observed shortest effective hold**, not by a proposed argument. Longer streams require a separate grant-linked, capacity-checked renewal/reconciliation mechanism; expiration may close a send-start window but must never make a potentially sent request eligible to send again.

The v361 activation revokes the admission LOGIN's direct amount-taking reserve calls, but retains its direct v350/v351 mark-dispatched and release calls. Those functions require `SESSION_USER=cinatoken_gateway_budget_admission`. A distinct grant LOGIN cannot call them as written; reusing the admission LOGIN leaves direct mark/release authority outside a durable grant. Grant activation therefore needs a coordinated wrapper and privilege cutover, with role-denial tests, so the grant transaction is the only way to establish the dispatched state for the new path.

## Required durable transitions

| Boundary | Required durable fact | Permitted physical action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v361 admitted, no grant | Quote ID, complete manifest, exact ordinary/Guardrail held amount and current deadlines | No send. |
| Grant claim transaction committed | Unique `(request_id, attempt_number)` with attempt number 1–3; exact manifest route/credential and prepared URL/body/secret commitments; current v359 route generation/attestation; all effective holds; server-clock send-start deadline; unresolved result obligation | At most one independent holder can begin the matched send before the deadline. |
| Grant COMMIT or connection-close acknowledgement lost | Claim may exist, so slot and potential egress are unknown until an authoritative DB read and holder evidence resolve them | No blind retry of that slot and no send from the ambiguous caller. |
| Holder begins or may have begun fetch | Persisted one-shot send-start/unknown state survives Worker death; use the same attempt identity for provider evidence, buyer settlement and economic outbox | Do not restore send rights when grant, holder lease or hold expires. |
| Provider outcome/charge uncertain | Retain `unknown` and budget/economic obligation until bill evidence or bounded manual resolution | Later attempts, if policy permits, consume new slots and stay within the already held three-attempt ceiling. |

The [v359 source fence](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/authenticated-text-route-source-fence-v359.sql) advances its generation on route, provider, pool, endpoint and link row mutations. v361 checks that generation at admission. A later grant must recheck it inside the grant transaction and the independent sender must verify the grant's source/credential identity against what it will actually use immediately before fetch. The [local Worker gate](../../../../packages/proxy/src/services/chat-text-grant-egress-contract-v361.ts) checks receipt shape and closes on uncertain acknowledgement, but it is unused and cannot prove an independently held secret or durable state.

These transitions were design requirements for the subsequent [v362 grant proposal](./C04-complete-text-attempt-grant-v362.md), which now has an isolated native fixture. It remains review-only with no deployed secret holder, renewal or economic reconciliation, and C04 remains unchecked.

## Live route mismatch in the local gate

The [normal route mapper](../../../../packages/proxy/src/services/model-router.ts) sets `providerKeyId` to `provider.id` even for platform credentials. At audit time the [v361 gate](../../../../packages/proxy/src/services/chat-text-grant-egress-contract-v361.ts) rejected every non-null `providerKeyId`, while its synthetic test route used `null`; this would reject an ordinary platform route before any broker claim. The local gate now requires `providerKeyId === providerId` and the test fixture uses that real route shape. The [Worker provider decorator](../../../../packages/proxy/src/runtime/workers.ts) also decrypts the stored Provider ciphertext before routing, whereas the local gate still requires `enc:v2:` source bytes for the v360 manifest hash. A real Chat route therefore still fails closed. Credential-opaque route planning and an independent holder remain outstanding.
