# C04 v363 grant result and lease boundary (design audit only)

2026-09-25. This is a review-only design audit, not SQL, an installed protocol, a physical send grant, or C04 acceptance. The [v362 proposal](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/complete-text-attempt-grant-v362.sql) records an immutable `unknown` attempt before a possible fetch and initially dispatches its exact v361 holds for 15 minutes. Its short `send_expires_at` is a send-start deadline; `hold_recovery_expires_at` is financial recovery time, not permission to start or continue a send. No independent secret holder, trusted outcome writer, grant-linked renewal, or platform economic closer is installed.

## Existing lifecycle and the gap

The [v350 ordinary mark](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/budget-admission-login-v350.sql) returns `true` for an already dispatched row without extending or proving its existing deadline covers a requested longer lease. The [v351 Guardrail mark](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/guardrail-budget-admission-login-v351.sql) rejects a longer dispatched replay. The [v355 extension](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/guardrail-budget-dispatched-extension-login-v355.sql) requires a BYOK `gateway_key_route` row; v361's platform Guardrail rows have `charged` basis. These are not v362 lease-renewal APIs. v362 also revokes direct v350/v351 mark execution from the admission LOGIN, so renewal needs its own narrowly authorized entry point.

The [v354 ordinary recovery](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/ordinary-budget-recovery-login-v354.sql) and [v353 Guardrail recovery](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/guardrail-budget-lifecycle-login-v353.sql) independently scan expired leases. For an expired **dispatched** reservation, each sets `settled_micros` to the *full reserved ceiling* and `state='expired'`: ordinary recovery subtracts reserved budget and adds the ceiling to user `budget_spent` when the account still has the reservation's budget epoch; Guardrail recovery subtracts window reserved and adds the ceiling to window settled. This is full-ceiling **forfeiture**, not a release or proof of actual provider usage. Only an expired **reserved, never dispatched** row becomes `released` with zero settlement. Uncoordinated forfeiture can split ordinary/Guardrail terminal states and has no grant-linked buyer/economic result. It cannot serve as the normal end of a long SSE or unknown attempt.

The existing [shared-key economic producer](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/shared-key-economic-producer-v2.sql) and [outbox](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/shared-key-economic-outbox.sql) require shared-key quote attempts and a `shared_key_usage_settled` event. They cannot represent a platform v362 grant. A generic buyer log or an event-delivery worker cannot resolve a physical send that lacks a grant-bound result and producer transaction.

## Minimum v363 review protocol

1. **Independent result facts.** A direct, separately provisioned `NOINHERIT` holder/result LOGIN, not the calling Worker, writes append-only facts keyed by `grant_id` and evidence nonce. Each fact echoes the immutable request, quote, attempt number/nonce, chosen route and credential identity, final-body digest, and both frozen outbound-body digests; the holder independently recomputes physical URL, upload, canonical transform, and plaintext credential fingerprint before send. Distinguish `fetch_not_called` supported by holder-side proof, authoritative provider-confirmed zero charge, observed usage/cost, and transport/provider unknown. A timeout, HTTP error, Worker report, or lost COMMIT/close ACK alone cannot prove zero billing. Conflicting or late evidence remains visible; it never rewrites the v362 grant or silently clears an unknown obligation.
2. **Atomic all-hold custody lease.** A grant-linked renewal function takes `grant_id` plus an expected lease epoch or nonce, uses database time, and serializes by request. It locks the grant, v361 admission, ordinary reservation when required, *every* exact Guardrail reservation and its window, and the ordinary account in one consistent order. It rechecks identity, amount, `dispatched` state, dispatch marker, complete row count, account/window reserved sums, and bounded integer arithmetic. It extends **all** held deadlines in one transaction to a bounded server-clock horizon (at most 15 minutes per renewal), records the new lease epoch and observed minimum expiry, or changes none. Zero-Guardrail and source-proven unlimited/no-ordinary-hold requests remain valid shapes with a result obligation. An API Key revocation after a committed send does not erase financial custody. No renewal extends the quote, v361 admission, v362 send-start deadline, or exhausted attempt slot.
3. **Live stream and recovery separation.** The independent holder heartbeats before its live lease runs out and stops further egress if it cannot obtain an acknowledged renewal. A recovery scanner renews a still-dispatched unknown obligation after Worker death for reconciliation, including a timestamp that has passed while the row remained dispatched; that recovery renewal never retroactively permits egress. Lease epoch and acknowledgement must be checked by the holder, so a lost renewal response cannot be assumed to extend its live send. Unknown obligations and held capacity remain durable until trusted resolution or an explicit terminal economic transaction.
4. **Reaper and terminal fences in the same activation.** v353/v354 expiry and explicit forfeit paths must skip grant-linked requests or enter a dedicated grant-aware closer. Database guards must reject independent `dispatched` terminal transitions, buyer logs, and ordinary/Guardrail settlements for a v362 request unless the same transaction carries a verified grant-linked terminal marker and typed economic event. The legacy reapers must continue processing unrelated requests instead of repeatedly failing on an enrolled row. If no closer is implemented in v363, the safe state is `dispatched` with reserved capacity retained even after the timestamp lapses, with no live send right. At a bounded hard horizon, a later closer must atomically choose evidenced actual amounts or conservative full-ceiling forfeiture for **both** budget systems, buyer settlement, and a platform grant outbox event; the existing separate v353/v354 functions and shared-key producer cannot do this transaction.
5. **Retry eligibility.** The current v362 function returns `pending_unknown` whenever any earlier grant exists. Permitting attempt 2 or 3 requires an exact-source change under the same per-request serialization lock. A previous attempt must have an independently verified terminal no-fetch/no-charge result and still-valid admission/route/hold conditions; ambiguous or billed attempts remain ineligible for this minimal protocol. A new grant consumes a new immutable slot and nonce, never replays an earlier row as a fresh send right, and remains within the original three-attempt reserved ceiling. Supporting retries after billed attempts requires a separate per-attempt aggregation and economic settlement design.

## Required native evidence before activation

- Real LOGIN/ACL tests: only the independent holder can write result facts or renew; Worker, runtime, admission, recovery, and buyer roles cannot forge facts or bypass terminal fences. Replay of one evidence nonce is idempotent; conflicting facts are retained/rejected according to a documented precedence rule.
- Lost grant or result COMMIT acknowledgement, holder crash before and after fetch, 429/timeout, late provider bill, and duplicate result must leave a durable unknown or exactly one supported terminal interpretation. No result may attach to a different grant, route, credential, attempt, or frozen body.
- Long SSE crosses the initial 15-minute lease through acknowledged renewals. A missed heartbeat stops egress; recovery retains all reserved capacity without granting a new send. Quote/admission/grant expiry never revives send-start authority.
- Concurrent renewal versus each v353/v354 reaper, explicit forfeit, and buyer writer must end with all holds still dispatched or one atomic grant-linked terminal outcome. One missing, changed, expired/terminal, or counter-inconsistent ordinary or Guardrail row rejects renewal without partial extension. Legacy non-grant requests still expire normally.
- Retry 2/3 requires trusted no-fetch/no-charge evidence and a fresh valid claim; ambiguous outcome, lost ACK, duplicate nonce, expired authority, and attempt 4 all fail closed. Verify unlimited/no-intent cases still retain the grant/result obligation.

Passing an isolated native PostgreSQL fixture would establish database invariants only. Production send authority additionally requires an independent credential holder, physical-upload proof, supervised long-stream cancellation, grant-aware economic settlement, and a cutover that installs all fences together.
