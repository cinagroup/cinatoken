# C04 v370 renewed holder result facts

2026-09-25. Review only; this is not a formal migration or an activation of the text holder. The [SQL successor](../../../../packages/core/migrations-proposals/postgres/complete-text-renewed-holder-facts-v370.sql) runs after v366 result facts and v367 all-hold renewal. It replaces the private v366 common fact writer in one migrator transaction. The existing holder and independent bill wrappers keep their signatures and permissions; no fact table row is rewritten and no new monetary or physical-send authority is granted.

The v365 custody and send-start identify the original physical send at epoch 1. For a holder observation claiming a later epoch, the writer takes the request advisory lock before locking the grant, then requires an append-only v367 renewal row for the same grant, request, holder run, and send-start. It writes the claimed committed epoch into the existing fact ledger. A missing epoch or wrong run is rejected. An older committed epoch remains receivable after a newer renewal so a delayed observation is not discarded. Reusing a nonce with another epoch is a conflict, not an idempotent replay. Provider-bill rows remain tied to the original send epoch because the bill actor does not claim holder renewal authority.

The unchanged v366 conflict table identifies a nonce/kind/digest conflict but has no attempted epoch field. A new private, append-only `complete_text_result_epoch_conflicts_v370` side ledger records the original fact ID, recorded and attempted epochs, source kinds, kinds, and digests for a cross-epoch replay. Its unique key includes the attempted epoch: repeating the same attempt is idempotent, while attempts at epochs 2 and 3 remain distinct even when v366's old conflict key collapses them. No evidence body or runtime table privilege is added. The side ledger rejects UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE.

The [owned PostgreSQL 18.6 fixture](../../../../scripts/db/cutover/postgres-complete-text-all-hold-renewal-v367.native.test.mjs) installs formal PG73 and the review proposals under separate direct LOGINs. Its [machine report](./C04-complete-text-all-hold-renewal-v367-report.json) records **38/38 stages and cleanup PASS**. The added stages check default-off installation and rollback, private side-ledger ACL and immutability, an epoch-3 holder fact through the existing direct client after COMMIT and connection-close acknowledgements, exact replay, missing epoch and wrong-run rejection, an older committed epoch-2 observation, distinct same-nonce/digest conflicts attempted at epochs 2 and 3, and an independent bill fact still stored at epoch 1. The fixture and SQL source SHA-256 values are in that report.

These are observation facts, not trusted billing decisions. PostgreSQL can confirm a renewal committed but cannot prove the holder received its COMMIT and close acknowledgements; the local holder must keep enforcing its last acknowledged deadline. A holder zero-charge observation remains unverified. The bill writer still depends on external source authentication, and neither this successor nor v366 resolves an unknown grant, closes all holds, emits a platform economic event, handles late bills, or fences facts after a terminal. The separate no-fetch fence and full result closer require a coordinated successor. C04.1–8 and C04.G remain unchecked.
