- Asset version: `2.1.1`.
- Original: `assets/logo/cinagroup-logo-rounded.png` (256 × 256).

The supplied pixels, proportions, colors and corners are preserved. Headers and
footer branding use the symbol without a CSS-drawn letter or adjacent wordmark.

context and product copy remain ordinary interface text.
